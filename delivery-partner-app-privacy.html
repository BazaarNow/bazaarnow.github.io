BazaarNow - Delivery Partner App Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how Bazaarindia Technologies Private Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of India and having its registered office at Hari Krupa HIG-B 483, 1st Stage Lakshmikanthnagar, Hebbal Layout, Mysore, Karnataka, 570016 (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects personal information of riders and delivery partners using the BazaarNow - Delivery Partner App (“Platform”).

By accessing or using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy, the Rider Agreement, Terms of Service, Intellectual Property Protection Terms, and Goods Protection Terms.

For additional support or inquiries, please visit our Contact Us page.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Rider Information

We may collect the following information from riders and onboarding applicants:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Government-issued identity documents
  • Selfie and profile photographs
  • Vehicle information
  • Bank account and payment details
  • Delivery and operational information
  • Communications with support teams

1.2 Background Location Information

With your consent, BazaarNow - Delivery Partner App may collect precise and background location information, including when the application is closed or not actively in use, for:

  • Live order tracking
  • Delivery verification
  • Route optimization
  • Order assignment
  • Fraud prevention and detection
  • Safety and operational monitoring
  • Customer order visibility

Disabling background location access may affect the functionality of the application and may prevent riders from receiving or completing orders.

1.3 Camera and File Permissions

The Platform may request access to camera, photos, media, and files for:

  • Selfie verification
  • KYC document uploads
  • Identity verification
  • Proof of delivery uploads
  • Incident reporting

BazaarNow only accesses files selected by the rider and does not access unrelated personal files or media.

1.4 Notification Permissions

Notifications may be used to send riders operational and transactional alerts including:

  • Assigned delivery orders
  • Order updates and status changes
  • OTP verification
  • Payment and incentive updates
  • Operational announcements
  • Security alerts

1.5 Physical Activity Permission

Physical activity recognition may be used to improve location accuracy, delivery movement tracking, and operational reliability during active deliveries.

1.6 Device and Technical Information

When you use the Platform, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • Application usage data
  • Crash reports and diagnostics
  • Device identifiers
  • Session timestamps and activity logs

2. PURPOSE OF DATA COLLECTION

We collect and use rider information for purposes including:

  • Rider onboarding and account verification
  • Order assignment and fulfilment
  • Delivery tracking and proof of delivery
  • Fraud detection and prevention
  • Customer support and dispute resolution
  • Payment processing and incentives
  • Operational analytics and performance monitoring
  • Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Safety and security investigations

3. BATTERY OPTIMIZATION

BazaarNow may request riders to disable battery optimization settings for reliable app functionality including:

  • Continuous background location tracking
  • Real-time order synchronization
  • Prevention of missed delivery requests
  • Reliable operational updates

Restricting background activity or enabling aggressive battery optimization settings may impact app performance and delivery operations.

4. DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

We may share rider information on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Customers during active deliveries
  • Store and vendor partners
  • Payment gateways and financial institutions
  • KYC and identity verification providers
  • Technology and cloud infrastructure providers
  • Government or regulatory authorities where required by law
  • Law enforcement agencies

BazaarNow does not sell rider personal information to third parties for independent marketing purposes.
